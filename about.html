About Hyper Roof, a Miami-Dade Roofing Contractor

Hyper Roof provides roofing services for property owners across Miami-Dade County, Florida. Our work focuses on roof repair, roof replacement, roof inspections and roofing concerns related to severe weather.

Every roofing project is different. The appropriate work depends on the roof system, the condition of the existing materials, the property and applicable local requirements. Contact our team to discuss the issue, arrange a project review and request an estimate.

We aim to communicate clearly about the proposed scope of work, materials, project timing and costs before a customer decides whether to proceed.
