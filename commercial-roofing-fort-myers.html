Commercial Roofing in Fort Myers, FL

Commercial roofing projects require planning around building use, roof system, drainage, access, tenant operations and applicable code requirements. Hyper Roof is an independent roofing information and contractor referral website serving Fort Myers.

Commercial roofing needs

  • Roof leak investigation and repair.
  • Roof replacement planning.
  • Preventive maintenance and inspections.
  • Storm damage assessment.
  • Roofing for offices, retail and other commercial properties.

What to include in a commercial roofing scope

Ask for the roof area, proposed system, preparation and tear-off assumptions, insulation or decking work, flashing, drainage, warranties, permits, inspections, schedule and exclusions. A clear scope makes competing bids easier to compare.

Fort Myers storm planning

Severe weather can disrupt commercial operations. A documented inspection and maintenance plan can help property managers identify developing problems before they become emergency issues.
