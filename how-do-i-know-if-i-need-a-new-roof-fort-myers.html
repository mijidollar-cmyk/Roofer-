FORT MYERS ROOF REPLACEMENT

How Do I Know If I Need a New Roof?

A Fort Myers roof does not need replacement simply because it reaches a certain age. Condition, storm history, recurring leaks and the remaining performance of the roofing system are more useful indicators.

Warning signs to investigate

  • Repeated or unexplained leaks.
  • Widespread deterioration or damaged roof coverings.
  • Storm or wind damage across multiple areas.
  • Persistent moisture, staining or damaged decking.
  • Roof components that are repeatedly failing despite repairs.
  • A roofing system approaching the end of its expected service life.

Repair versus replacement

A small, isolated defect may be a repair. Replacement becomes more reasonable when damage is widespread, repairs are recurring, the underlying assembly is compromised or the roof is no longer performing reliably.

Get an inspection before deciding

A professional inspection can document visible conditions and help define the actual scope. Ask for photos, a written scope, material specifications and a clear explanation of repair and replacement alternatives.

Fort Myers storm exposure

Severe weather can create damage that is difficult to see from ground level. If a significant storm has passed, an inspection can be useful even when interior leaks have not appeared.
