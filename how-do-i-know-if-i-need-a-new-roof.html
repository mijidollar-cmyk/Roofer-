How Do I Know If I Need a New Roof?

A roof does not always need replacement because of one damaged shingle. In Miami, a professional assessment can help determine whether repair is practical or replacement is the better long-term option.

Signs replacement may be worth considering

  • Frequent or recurring roof leaks.
  • Widespread damaged, missing or deteriorated roofing materials.
  • A roof that is approaching the expected service life for its material.
  • Storm damage across multiple areas of the roof.
  • Repair needs that continue to grow despite previous work.

Repair or replacement?

The decision depends on roof age, damage, material, installation quality and the cost of continuing repairs. A roof inspection can identify the likely cause and the practical next step.
