FORT MYERS ROOF INSPECTIONS

How Often Should You Inspect a Roof in Florida?

There is no single inspection schedule that fits every Fort Myers property. Roof condition, material, age, maintenance history and severe-weather exposure all matter.

Good times to schedule an inspection

  • After a major storm or high-wind event.
  • When a leak, ceiling stain or moisture problem appears.
  • When the roof shows visible deterioration.
  • Before buying or selling a property.
  • When the roof is older or repeated repairs are occurring.

What an inspection should accomplish

An inspection should identify visible defects and areas that deserve further evaluation. Ask for photographs, a written summary and clear recommendations rather than a vague statement that the roof is “good” or “bad.”

Do not inspect a damaged roof yourself

Wet, steep or storm-damaged roofs can be hazardous. A ground-level visual check can be useful for homeowners, but climbing onto a roof should be left to appropriately qualified professionals.

Fort Myers weather matters

Heat, rain, humidity and severe weather can accelerate wear or expose existing weaknesses. Post-storm inspection can be especially valuable when wind or flying debris may have affected the roof.
