ROOF INSPECTION GUIDE

How Often Should You Inspect a Roof in Florida?

Regular roof inspections can help identify developing problems before they become larger repairs. The right schedule depends on roof age, condition, material and exposure to severe weather.

When an inspection makes sense

  • As part of routine roof maintenance.
  • After a major storm or high-wind event.
  • When you notice leaks, stains or damaged roofing materials.
  • Before buying or selling a property.
  • When an older roof begins showing repeated problems.

Why post-storm inspections matter

Some storm damage is not obvious from the ground. A professional can assess visible roofing materials and identify areas that may require repair or further evaluation.
