FORT MYERS STORM GUIDE

How to Handle Hurricane Roof Damage in Fort Myers

After a hurricane or severe storm, the priority is safety and preventing additional damage. Roof problems can range from missing materials and damaged flashing to water intrusion that becomes visible hours later.

First steps after a storm

  1. Stay away from visibly unstable roof areas and downed electrical equipment.
  2. Check the interior for active leaks and move valuables away from water.
  3. Use safe temporary protection where practical; do not climb onto a damaged roof.
  4. Photograph and document visible damage.
  5. Arrange a professional roof inspection when conditions are safe.

What an inspection can look for

A qualified roofing professional can inspect roof coverings, flashing, penetrations, drainage, exposed underlayment and other accessible components. Damage is not always visible from the ground, so avoid assuming that a roof is unaffected simply because there are no missing shingles or tiles.

Insurance and storm damage

If you plan to make a claim, document conditions before non-emergency repairs and follow your insurer's instructions. Coverage depends on the policy and circumstances; no contractor should guarantee claim approval.

When is roof damage an emergency?

Active water entry, a large exposed roof area, major structural concerns or conditions that make the property unsafe deserve prompt attention. Use emergency measures to protect the building while arranging professional repair.
