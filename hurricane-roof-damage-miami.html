MIAMI STORM DAMAGE

Hurricane Roof Damage Repair in Miami, FL

Hurricanes and severe storms can expose a roof to wind, rain, flying debris, and water intrusion. After a storm, prompt roof assessment can help you identify damage and protect your property from additional problems.

What to do after storm damage

  • Stay off the roof and avoid unsafe areas
  • Look for visible leaks or water intrusion from a safe location
  • Document visible damage with photos when it is safe to do so
  • Arrange a professional roof inspection when damage is suspected
  • Address active leaks quickly to limit interior and structural damage

Storm roof repair or replacement?

Not every storm-damaged roof needs full replacement. The right solution depends on the extent of the damage and the condition of the existing roofing system. Hyper Roof can inspect the problem and explain the repair or replacement options.
