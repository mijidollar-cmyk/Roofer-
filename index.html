FAQ

Roofing Quote & Service Questions

What information should I include in a quote request?

Provide your name, phone number, property ZIP code, roof type if known and a short description of the work or problem. Photos or additional project details can be discussed through the appropriate follow-up channel.

Does submitting the form guarantee a roofing contractor or price?

No. A request is an inquiry only. It does not guarantee contractor availability, a quote, a price, financing, insurance coverage or acceptance of the project.

How can I compare roofing contractors?

Ask for current licensing and insurance information, a written scope of work, material details, permit responsibilities, warranty terms and an expected timeline. Verify important information independently before hiring.

What should I do after storm damage?

Prioritize safety, document visible damage from a safe location when possible, protect the property from further exposure where appropriate and arrange a professional assessment. Avoid unsafe roof access.

What roofing materials are common in Miami?

Common systems include asphalt shingles, concrete or clay tile, metal roofing and several flat-roof systems. The appropriate choice depends on the property, design, budget, applicable requirements and qualified professional advice.

Do I need a permit for roof work?

Permit requirements vary by project and jurisdiction. Ask the contractor which permits apply and who is responsible for obtaining them before work begins.
