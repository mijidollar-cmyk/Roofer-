FAQ

Frequently Asked Roofing Questions

How often should I inspect my roof in Miami?

We recommend a professional roof inspection at least once a year and after major storms. Regular inspections can help identify leaks, damaged materials, and other problems early.

How do I know if I need roof repair or replacement?

Leaks, missing or damaged roofing materials, recurring repairs, visible deterioration, and an aging roof can all be signs that your property needs professional evaluation.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most residential replacements take a few days, depending on the roof size, materials, weather, access, and site conditions.

Do you offer emergency roofing in Miami?

Yes. Hyper Roof provides urgent response for active leaks and storm-related roof damage so you can protect your property and plan the next repair.
