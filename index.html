FAQ

Fort Myers Roofing Questions

When should I inspect my Fort Myers roof?

Consider routine inspections, inspections after significant storms, and an inspection whenever you notice leaks or visible deterioration.

Does Lee County require a roof permit?

Lee County roofing guidance states that permits are required for roof replacement, repair or re-cover work on existing structures. Confirm current requirements for your specific project.

Should I repair or replace my roof?

The answer depends on the extent of damage, roof condition, repair history and remaining service life. A professional assessment can help define the scope.

Can you guarantee an insurance claim?

No. Insurance coverage is determined by the policy and circumstances. Any contractor should avoid guaranteeing claim approval.
