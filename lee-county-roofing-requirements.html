Lee County Roofing Requirements: What Fort Myers Homeowners Should Know

Roofing projects in Fort Myers should be planned around the applicable Florida Building Code and Lee County requirements. Requirements can change, so confirm current rules with the authority having jurisdiction before work begins.

Roof permits

Lee County's roof application and permitting guidance states that a permit is required to replace, repair or re-cover a roof on an existing structure. The exact application, documents and inspections depend on the project.

Roof systems and engineering

Some roofing systems can require additional engineering or documentation. For example, Lee County guidance notes special considerations when tile roofing is installed on a structure that did not previously have tile.

Roof-to-wall connections

Lee County guidance also identifies additional roof-to-wall connection requirements that can apply to certain conventionally built single-family residences, subject to stated conditions and exceptions. A contractor or design professional should confirm whether your property falls within those requirements.

Why homeowners should verify current rules

Permit requirements depend on project type, building characteristics and current code. Use official Lee County and Florida Building Code resources rather than relying on an old article or a generic roofing website.

Before hiring a roofer

  • Ask what permits and inspections apply.
  • Request a written scope of work.
  • Verify licensing and insurance.
  • Confirm who obtains the permit.
  • Keep permit and inspection records.
