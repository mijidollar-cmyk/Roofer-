Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Hyper Roof is a roofing-information website focused on Miami-Dade County, Florida. The website includes an optional quote-request form. The form asks for your name, phone number, property ZIP code, roof type and optional project details. The site also uses hosting infrastructure that may process technical request logs for delivery, reliability and security.

1. Information you provide

When you submit the quote-request form, the information you provide is sent to the site's server-side form handler. When the email service is configured, the handler sends the submission to the quote-request email destination configured by the site operator through the Resend email delivery service. The current form handler does not write submissions to a website database. Do not include payment card details, passwords, government ID numbers or other sensitive information in the project notes.

2. Contractor and service-provider sharing

The current form handler sends submissions to the quote-request email destination configured by the site operator. It does not currently contain an automated contractor-distribution or CRM integration. If information is later shared with a contractor or another provider, this policy should be updated to explain that sharing before it begins.

3. Affiliate relationships

Hyper Roof may receive compensation for contractors, leads, advertising, or other marketing activities. This compensation does not mean that Hyper Roof controls a contractor's handling of your information or guarantees a contractor's services.

4. Information collected automatically

The hosting platform may process technical information in server or security logs, such as IP address, browser type, requested URL, and request time, for delivery, reliability and security. No analytics or advertising script is present in the current website source. Confirm the hosting provider’s actual logging and retention practices before publication.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

The current website source does not intentionally set analytics or advertising cookies. The hosting platform or future third-party integrations may use cookies or similar technologies; update this policy if those are enabled.

6. How information is used

Information may be used to review and respond to quote requests, communicate about the roofing concern you describe, maintain website security, improve the website and comply with applicable law.

7. Sharing information

We may process information through the website hosting provider and the email delivery provider used to transmit quote requests. The form handler currently sends the submission to the configured quote-request inbox and does not include an automated contractor-sharing integration. We do not sell personal information as a standalone data product.

8. Third-party websites and contractors

Links, advertisements, contractor tools, and contractor listings may take you to third-party websites. Hyper Roof does not control those websites or contractors and is not responsible for their privacy practices, security, data handling, or conduct.

9. Data security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information handled through the website. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Your choices and requests

You may contact us with questions about information you provided or request access, correction, or deletion where applicable and legally permitted. You may also opt out of certain communications or adjust browser cookie settings.

11. Children

This website is intended for general audiences and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly request personal information from children under 13.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, use the website's Contact page. A dedicated privacy email or postal address has not been published; add a monitored contact address before collecting information at scale or enabling additional lead-sharing features.
