Roof Inspection in Fort Myers, FL

A professional roof inspection can help identify visible damage, maintenance needs and conditions that deserve further evaluation. It is especially useful after severe weather or when buying or selling a property.

When to consider an inspection

  • After a major storm or high-wind event.
  • When you notice a leak or ceiling stain.
  • When roof materials visibly deteriorate.
  • Before buying or selling a property.
  • When repeated repairs are occurring.

What homeowners should receive

Ask for a clear explanation of observed conditions, photographs where appropriate, recommended next steps and a written scope for any repair or replacement work. An inspection is not the same thing as a guarantee of future roof performance.

Safety first

Do not climb onto a wet, steep or storm-damaged roof. A professional should handle roof-level inspection when conditions present a fall or structural hazard.
