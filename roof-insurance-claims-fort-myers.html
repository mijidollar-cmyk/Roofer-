FORT MYERS INSURANCE GUIDE

Does Homeowners Insurance Cover Roof Damage in Fort Myers?

Coverage depends on the policy, the cause of loss, exclusions, deductibles and the condition of the roof. A storm does not automatically mean an insurance claim will be approved.

What to do after roof damage

  1. Protect people and avoid unsafe areas.
  2. Take photos and video of visible damage before making non-essential changes.
  3. Prevent additional damage where reasonably safe, and keep receipts for emergency mitigation.
  4. Review the policy and contact the insurer or claims representative.
  5. Arrange a professional roof assessment and keep copies of reports and estimates.

Document more than the roof

Photograph damaged ceilings, walls, flooring, gutters, screens and personal property as well as the roof. Record the date of the storm and keep correspondence, invoices and temporary-repair receipts.

Do not assume the contractor or insurer decides coverage

The insurer determines coverage under the policy. A roofing contractor can document physical conditions and prepare a scope or estimate, but should not promise that an insurer will pay a claim.

Fort Myers storm considerations

Southwest Florida properties can experience heavy rain and high winds. After a significant storm, an inspection can identify missing materials, lifted components, flashing problems or water entry that may not be obvious from the ground.

Questions to ask

Should I replace the roof before filing a claim?

Usually, document the damage and discuss the claim process with your insurer before committing to non-emergency replacement work, unless immediate safety or mitigation requires action.

Can a roofer guarantee insurance approval?

No. Coverage is determined under your insurance policy.
