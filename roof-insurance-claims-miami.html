Roof Insurance Claims in Miami, FL

Roof damage can create questions about documentation, repairs, and insurance. Coverage depends on your policy and the circumstances of the loss, so your insurance provider is the right source for coverage decisions.

Steps after roof damage

  1. Make safety your first priority and avoid unsafe roof areas.
  2. Document visible damage from a safe location with photos or video.
  3. Take reasonable temporary steps to prevent additional damage when it is safe to do so.
  4. Contact your insurance provider or claims representative for guidance on your policy.
  5. Arrange a professional roofing assessment to document the condition and identify needed repairs.
