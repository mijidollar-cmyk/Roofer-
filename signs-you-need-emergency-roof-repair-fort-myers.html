FORT MYERS EMERGENCY ROOFING

Signs You Need Emergency Roof Repair

Some roofing problems should not wait for a convenient appointment. In Fort Myers, severe weather can create active leaks, exposed roof areas and other conditions that need prompt attention.

Warning signs

  • Active water entering the building.
  • A large section of roof covering is missing or displaced.
  • Visible holes or exposed underlayment after a storm.
  • Major damage around flashing, vents or other penetrations.
  • Ceiling or interior damage that is rapidly worsening.
  • Possible structural instability or unsafe roof conditions.

What to do first

Keep people away from unsafe areas, move valuables away from water and use safe temporary protection where practical. Do not climb onto a wet or damaged roof.

Emergency repair versus permanent replacement

An emergency response may temporarily limit water entry or protect an exposed area. The permanent solution could be a localized repair or a full replacement depending on the underlying damage.

After a severe storm

Document the visible condition and contact your insurer if appropriate. A roofing professional can assess the roof when conditions are safe and provide a written scope of recommended work.
