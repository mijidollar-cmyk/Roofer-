EMERGENCY ROOFING GUIDE

Signs You Need Emergency Roof Repair

Some roofing problems should not wait for a routine appointment. Fast action can help reduce additional damage to the roof and interior of the property.

Common urgent warning signs

  • Active water entering the building.
  • Large areas of missing or severely damaged roofing materials.
  • Storm or wind damage that exposes the roof system.
  • Collapsed, sagging or visibly unstable roof areas.
  • Damage that is getting worse during ongoing rain.

What to do

Protect people and property first. Avoid unsafe roof access and electrical hazards around water. Document visible damage when safe, then arrange professional emergency roofing assistance.
