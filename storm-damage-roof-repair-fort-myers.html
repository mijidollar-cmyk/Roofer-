FORT MYERS STORM DAMAGE

Storm Damage Roof Repair in Fort Myers, FL

Severe weather can damage roofing materials, flashing, vents and other components without making every problem obvious from the ground. Prompt assessment can help limit additional water intrusion and define the repair scope.

Common storm-related problems

  • Missing or displaced roof coverings.
  • Damaged flashing and penetrations.
  • Water intrusion and interior staining.
  • Debris or impact damage.
  • Exposed underlayment or roof areas.

What to do after a storm

Prioritize safety, document visible conditions, protect the interior where safe and arrange a professional assessment. Do not climb onto a damaged roof.

Repair or replacement?

The correct scope depends on the extent and location of damage and the condition of the existing system. Ask for photographs and a written scope rather than accepting a blanket recommendation without explanation.

Insurance

If you are considering an insurance claim, document damage and follow your insurer's claim instructions. Coverage is determined by the policy and circumstances; no contractor can guarantee approval.
