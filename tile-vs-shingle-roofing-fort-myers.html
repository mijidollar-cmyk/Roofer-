FORT MYERS ROOF TYPES

Tile vs. Shingle Roofing in Fort Myers

Tile and shingle systems can both be appropriate for Fort Myers homes. The better choice depends on the structure, budget, appearance, maintenance expectations, installation requirements and selected products.

Tile roofing

Tile can provide a distinctive appearance and long service life when correctly specified and installed. The structure must be suitable for the system, and damaged individual tiles may require careful replacement.

Shingle roofing

Asphalt shingles are common because they offer a broad range of styles and price points. Performance depends on the product, installation method, ventilation, exposure and maintenance.

How should you compare them?

  • Expected service life and manufacturer requirements.
  • Wind performance and applicable installation requirements.
  • Weight and structural suitability.
  • Up-front project cost and future maintenance.
  • Appearance and neighborhood considerations.
  • Availability of qualified installation and repair.

Do not choose from appearance alone

Ask a roofing professional to confirm that the selected system is appropriate for the existing structure and local requirements. The product, underlayment, flashing and installation details matter as much as the visible covering.
