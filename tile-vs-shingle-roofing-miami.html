Tile vs. Shingle Roofing in Miami

Tile and shingle roofs can both be suitable for Miami properties. The best choice depends on the building, roof structure, budget, appearance and expected maintenance.

Tile roofing

Tile can offer a distinctive appearance and long service life when properly selected and installed. Its weight and installation requirements should be evaluated for the specific structure.

Shingle roofing

Shingles are a common residential roofing option and can offer a practical balance of appearance, installation options and cost. Product quality and proper installation matter greatly in Florida weather.

How to choose

Consider the existing roof structure, local requirements, climate exposure, desired service life, maintenance expectations and total project cost. A professional roof assessment can help narrow the options.
