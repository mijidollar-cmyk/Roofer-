What to Do When Your Roof Starts Leaking

A roof leak can worsen quickly during heavy rain. Your first goal is to protect people and limit interior damage, then arrange a professional assessment to find the source.

Immediate steps

  1. Move furniture and valuables away from the leak.
  2. Use containers to catch water where safe.
  3. Document stains, dripping, damaged materials and visible exterior damage.
  4. Do not climb onto a wet or damaged roof.
  5. Arrange professional roof leak repair as soon as conditions permit.

Why the leak location can be misleading

Water can travel along roof layers, framing or other building components before appearing indoors. The spot where you see water is not necessarily the point where the roof covering failed.

When is it an emergency?

Active water intrusion, a large exposed roof area, electrical hazards, structural concerns or rapidly worsening damage should be treated as urgent. Use safe temporary measures while waiting for professional help.

Repair or replacement?

A professional inspection should determine whether the issue is localized or part of a broader roofing problem. Ask for a written scope and photos so you can understand the recommended work.
