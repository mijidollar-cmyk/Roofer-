ROOF LEAK GUIDE

What to Do When Your Roof Starts Leaking

An active roof leak can worsen quickly, especially during heavy Miami rain. Focus first on protecting people and property, then arrange a professional assessment.

Immediate steps

  1. Keep people away from unsafe areas and avoid electrical hazards around wet ceilings or fixtures.
  2. Move furniture and belongings away from the affected area.
  3. Use containers to catch water where safe and practical.
  4. Take photos or video of visible damage for your records.
  5. Arrange professional roof leak repair or an inspection as soon as possible.

Do not ignore a small leak

Water can travel through roofing, insulation and ceilings before becoming visible. Repeated leaks can also cause additional property damage. The source should be identified rather than simply covering the interior stain.
