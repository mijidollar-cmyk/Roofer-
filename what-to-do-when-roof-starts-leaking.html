ROOF LEAK GUIDE

What to Do Immediately When Your Miami Roof Starts Leaking

A roof leak can get worse quickly, especially during heavy Florida rain. If you are searching for what to do when your roof starts leaking, focus first on safety, limiting interior damage and documenting the condition.

6 steps to take when your roof leaks

  1. Protect people first. Keep clear of wet areas, unstable ceilings and any electrical hazards.
  2. Contain water indoors. Use containers and move furniture or valuables away from the leak when safe.
  3. Document the damage. Photograph or video the affected ceiling, walls, belongings and visible roof damage from a safe location.
  4. Limit further damage when safe. If temporary protection is necessary, use a qualified professional rather than climbing onto a wet or damaged roof.
  5. Contact your insurer if appropriate. Keep records and receipts for emergency measures.
  6. Arrange roof repair. A professional assessment can identify the source and determine whether repair or replacement is appropriate.

Do not climb onto a leaking roof

Wet roofs, storm debris and damaged structures can be dangerous. If the roof is steep, visibly damaged or affected by a storm, stay off it and arrange a professional inspection.

Is a roof leak an emergency?

Active water entering the building, a sagging ceiling, a large opening, major storm damage or exposed roof components can require urgent attention. See our signs you need emergency roof repair.

Repair or replacement?

A localized leak may be repairable. Multiple leaks, widespread deterioration or major storm damage may justify a replacement assessment. Read our Miami roof repair guide and roof replacement guide.

Frequently asked questions

Can I fix a roof leak myself?

Temporary interior measures may reduce damage, but climbing onto a roof can be dangerous. A professional should diagnose and permanently repair the roof.

Should I take pictures before repairing a leak?

Yes, when it is safe. Photos and video can help document the condition for your records and, if applicable, an insurance claim.

Will insurance cover a roof leak?

Coverage depends on the policy and cause of loss. Contact your insurer for a coverage decision.
